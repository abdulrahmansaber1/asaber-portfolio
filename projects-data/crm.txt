Corevine Portfolio

-------------------
Project Title: Corevine Travel CRM
Project Overview: A comprehensive Customer Relationship Management (CRM) and Reservation system designed specifically for the travel and hospitality industry. This application streamlines the entire booking lifecycle, from initial client inquiry to reservation management (Hotels, Cars, Airport Transfers) and financial processing (Invoicing, Payments). It serves as a centralized hub for managing clients, agents, and service providers.

Key Features:

Multi-Vertical Reservation System: specialized modules for managing Hotel, Car Rental, and Airport Transfer reservations with detailed tracking.
Financial Management Suite: Robust handling of Invoices, Payments, Expenses, and Currencies, including automated rate calculations.
Inventory & Logistics:
Hotels: Management of rooms, meal plans, and aging reports.
Transport: Fleet management including internal/external car logs and driver assignments.
Document Automation: Automated generation of PDF Vouchers and Invoices for clients and service providers.
CRM Core: Dedicated management for Clients, Companies, and Agents to maintain strong business relationships.
Audit & Security: Integrated Activity Logging to track user actions and history for security and accountability.
Dashboard & Analytics: Statistical overview of business performance.
Tech Stack:

Layer	Technologies
Frontend	Next.js, Tailwind CSS
Backend	Laravel 11, PHP 8.2
Database	MySQL
Authentication	Laravel Sanctum
Key Libraries	Spatie Activitylog (Auditing), DomPDF (PDF Generation)

Corevine Travel CRM

A full-stack CRM and Reservation platform engineered for the travel industry. The system unifies complex booking workflows across hotels, car rentals, and airport transfers into a single intuitive interface.

Key Features:

Unified Booking Engine: Seamlessly manages reservations for Hotels, Cars, and Transfers.
Financial Operations: Integrated invoicing, multi-currency support, and expense tracking.
Automated Workflows: Instant generation of PDF vouchers and invoices.
Fleet & Inventory: Detailed tracking of drivers, vehicles, and hotel room inventory.
My Tech Stack: Built with a modern Next.js and Tailwind CSS frontend for a responsive, high-performance user experience, backed by a robust Laravel 11 API.

Backend: Laravel 11, PHP 8.2, Sanctum, MySQL
