Project: Nursy
Nursy is a comprehensive healthcare platform designed to bridge the gap between patients and professional nurses, facilitating on-demand and scheduled care services. The system manages the entire service lifecycle—from booking and nurse verification to real-time communication and secure financial transactions—ensuring a seamless experience for all users.

Key Features
Multi-Role Ecosystem: Distinct, secure workflows for Patients, Nurses, Custodians, and Administrators.
Advanced Booking System: Supports both "OnSpot" (immediate) and "Scheduled" requests with granular service customization.
Nurse Verification Pipeline: Robust system for uploading and validating nurse credentials (licenses, IDs, CVs) to ensure quality of care.
Real-Time Interaction: Integrated chat functionality powered by WebSockets for instant communication between patients and nurses.
Financial Infrastructure: Complete wallet system with credit/debit history and secure payment gateway integration (Kashier).
Geolocation Services: Location-based logic to efficiently match patients with nearby available nurses.
